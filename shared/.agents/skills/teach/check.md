# Check

The user brought their own explanation, summary, or notes. Their synthesis, corrected, is worth more than yours.

1. Ask calibration on the whole piece. Done when a number is given.
2. Mark, quoting short pieces, everything **missing**, **wrong**, or **unsupported** (asserted without a reason). Do not rewrite their text. Done when every claim in the piece has been judged.
3. Ask for a revision; check the revision the same way. Done when no wrong or unsupported claim remains; then enter what was wrong in the ledger (`thread.md`, Ledger).
